🚗 Realistic vehicle model
📏 Mileage for every vehicle – saved for owned cars, optional odometer above the minimap.
🛢️ 11 service parts with km intervals – oil, air / fuel filter, spark plugs, coolant, brake fluid, transmission fluid, brake pads, tyres, timing belt, EV battery coolant.
⚙️ 13 core parts – engine, transmission, clutch, radiator, alternator, battery, fuel pump, brakes, suspension, alignment, turbo, EV battery, EV motor.
⚠️ Neglect has consequences – an overdue service starts damaging the linked core parts, a worn-out timing belt snaps.
💥 Collisions by impact zone – a frontal crash hits the radiator and engine, a side hit the suspension and alignment.
🌡️ Engine temperature – warning, power cut, then the engine shuts down until it cools.
⚡ Electric vehicles – own parts, no oil or timing belt, extra drain from a worn battery.
🕵️ NPC & stolen cars come with a random history (20 000–250 000 km). Cars bought in a vehicle shop start brand new.

Part	Symptoms
Engine	power loss · misfires · stalling · smoke · won't start
Transmission / clutch	slow shifts · limp mode · clutch slip under throttle
Battery / alternator	slow cranking · flickering lights · dead battery
Brakes / pads / fluid	longer braking distance
Suspension / alignment	wobble · pulling to one side · lower top speed
Tyres	less grip · blowouts at high speed
Radiator / coolant	overheating · steam from the engine bay
